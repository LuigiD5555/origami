# Origami project boundary

```text
TLALOC                              ORIGAMI
work/orchestration system           generative representation/state-machine language
behavior compilation       ----->   semantic/representation contracts
Tlaloque coordination      ----->   reference/projection contracts
verification/evaluation             Fold/Unfold, state, relations, dynamics, observation
```

Origami may be used independently of Tlaloc. Tlaloc may operate without Origami or consume Origami as one representation provider.

The stable rule is:

> Tlaloc coordinates work; Tlaloque perform bounded specialist work; Origami defines how information and state are folded into an addressable representation, related, transformed, observed and selectively unfolded.

## Origami core boundary

Origami core owns:

- generative Fold/Unfold semantics;
- representation nodes such as literals, references, repetition, transforms, rules, graphs, residuals and verification records when promoted into the core;
- stable addressing and selective unfolding contracts;
- state and relation semantics;
- deterministic reference transitions;
- observation contracts and perceptual-availability semantics;
- projection contracts.

A rasterized source is not automatically an Origami representation. `RASTER_IS_NOT_FOLD`: screenshots/page renders may be baselines, fixtures, ingestion artifacts or projections, but rasterization alone cannot establish generative Fold, semantic addressability or selective unfolding.

Within Origami, **OHF (Origami HyperFold)** is a carrier/protocol and executable research track. R3.10-LAB, DVR/PSS, Glyph Calculus, SAFE_MICRO_ISA and Context SIMD belong to that track unless and until a capability is deliberately generalized into the core Origami language.

OHF may project an established Origami representation into a compact/perceptual carrier and gather evidence about that carrier. Carrier success does not redefine the underlying Origami semantics.

Origami owns its semantic contracts. Tlaloc may build fixtures, evaluators and model-facing behavior around those contracts but must not redefine them.
