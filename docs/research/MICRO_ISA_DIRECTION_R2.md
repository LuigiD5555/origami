# MICRO-ISA direction under Architecture R2

Existing SAFE_MICRO_ISA / Context SIMD work is active evidence infrastructure for measuring which small visual/semantic operations and widths are actually reliable.

Under R2 it should be connected to, but not conflated with, Tonal T2 Primitive Swarm.

## Separation

Tonal T2 asks whether a small semantic capability basis composes into complex behavior.

Origami MICRO-ISA asks which representation/perceptual operations can be encoded and recovered safely under Origami-specific constraints.

A successful Origami micro-op may become a candidate carrier primitive, but it does not automatically become a universal Shponglese semantic primitive.

## Existing useful gates

Preserve principles such as:

- start narrow;
- widen only demonstrated winners;
- stop on false-known or exactness failure;
- measure retries/tokens/latency/cost;
- distinguish nominal capacity from safe measured capacity;
- do not promote before cross-run/model evidence where required.
